Company

Proudly South African to the world.

Exclusive Shipping Solutions (Pty) Ltd was founded in 2014 with one simple aim: to bring quality South African goods to buyers around the world, without the hassle, and at affordable prices to the end consumer.

Our team brings 20+ years of experience in international trade, logistics, manufacturing, and import and export customs. From the farm or factory floor to you.

From our head office in East London, South Africa, we work with in-house agents across the country. We make sure to source the right product straight from the factory or farm, at factory prices, so you get quality goods for the best deal.

We offer a true A to Z solution. We handle sourcing, manufacturing, customs, paperwork and shipping, all the way to the end consumer. You deal with one partner from factory to client.

Our promise is simple: best pricing, quality goods, reliable and on time.

That's why buyers choose Exclusive Shipping over anyone else.
